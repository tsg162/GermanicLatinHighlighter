Word Roots Highlighter

Paste English text and see which words come from Germanic, Latin, Norse, Greek and other roots.

Solid underline = recorded origin. Dashed outline = an inference or an unresolved origin. Words follow their historical roots through intermediary languages: philosophy counts as Greek, street as Latin, and Scots belongs to Germanic. Mixed formations and words with different origins for different meanings appear as Unresolved; hover for details. Lexical data: Wiktionary contributors via Kaikki, CC BY-SA 4.0.
